## MY FAVORITE SERIES AND MOVIES ##


I like stories that feel emotional and a little different. ***The Substance*** is about a woman who takes a drug to become young again, but it creates another version of her. it’s creepy and makes you think about identity.

***I Believe in Unicorns*** is more quiet and dreamy, about a teenage girl who goes on a road trip with a boy to escape her hard life, but things don’t go as planned. 

For series, ***Seraph of the end*** is an anime with vampires and action, where kids fight to survive in a broken world. 

***Rookie historian Goo Hae-ryung*** is a k-drama about a smart woman who becomes a palace historian and challenges old rules, with a mix of romance and history. i really connect with these kinds of stories.
